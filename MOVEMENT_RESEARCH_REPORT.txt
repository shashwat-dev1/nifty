Direction and point movement extension — 24 November 2026
Original KP engine and original ML outputs preserved. No revised evaluation on 2026.
Eight targets trained: 15/30/60/120/240-minute same-session, remaining session,
pre-market full-day open-to-close, next observed eligible session open-to-close.
Four comparisons per target: prior/mean baseline, market-only, KP-only, combined.
Three development evaluation years: 2023, 2024, 2025. Fixed hyperparameters.
Fit period ends before the prior calibration year with one-day label-end embargo;
calibration uses the prior year, also embargoed; evaluation uses following year.
Test labels are nonoverlapping. This is development replay, not untouched prospective evidence.
Classification: regularized logistic regression + chronological sigmoid calibration;
neutral +/-0.05 percent by default, configurable through movement_models.py --neutral.
Calibration is a procedure, not proof of calibrated future probabilities.
Point mean: ridge regression; median and lower/upper point movements use chronological
calibration residual quantiles. Nominal 5th/95th bounds have no guaranteed coverage.
Measured test interval coverage, MAE, RMSE, log loss, Brier and accuracy are saved.
Pre-market inputs use previous daily returns/volatility and 09:00 IST KP factors.
Actual same-day open and gap are excluded from inputs; open is used only in target.
Premarket estimated percent uses known previous close, not the unknown opening price.
Elapsed market inputs require an uninterrupted 30-minute lookback; labels require every
minute up to endpoint within one date. Session targets conservatively require complete
375-minute standard sessions with 09:16–15:30 close timestamps. Special sessions are
excluded pending authoritative historical calendar integration. Remaining-session sample
is drawn from eligible stored decision timestamps and excludes the last 15 minutes.
No forecasting service or persisted prospective model is approved. 2026+ selected dates
show no revised model predictions. Existing replay pages remain explicitly labelled.
Full per-year results: MOVEMENT_RESULTS.csv; datasets/predictions: archive/movement.
